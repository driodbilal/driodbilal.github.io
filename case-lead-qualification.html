Lead qualification for Aeon Digital

Every inquiry on the agency's website is checked by budget and answered automatically.

Service
Automation
Client
Aeon Digital, a digital agency
Built with
Jotform, Make.com, Calendly

The problem

Inquiries of every size came in, and the team spent time replying to leads that weren't a good fit.

How it works

Good-fit leads
Website form→Make.com→Budget $5k+→Email with Calendly link
Other leads
Website form→Make.com→Budget under $5k→Polite thank-you

What I built

  • A Jotform on the website that sends every submission to Make.com.
  • A filter that checks the marketing budget each lead enters.
  • Leads with $5k+ budgets get an email with a link to book a call.
  • Smaller leads get a polite thank-you saying the team is fully booked.

The result

Good-fit leads book calls straight away, and the team spends its time on the right clients.
