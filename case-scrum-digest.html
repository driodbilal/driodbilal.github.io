Daily scrum digest for an agency team

Claude checks the team's ClickUp every day and flags work that has gone quiet or got stuck.

The problem

With many clients and tasks, work went quiet without anyone noticing until a deadline was close.

How it works

ClickUp tasks→Claude checks every task→Flags quiet or stuck work→Daily digest→Live dashboard

What it flags

  • Tasks with no comment or update in 7 days.
  • Tasks stuck in the same status for 7 days.
  • Tasks waiting in review for 3 days or more.

What I built

  • A scheduled Claude task that reads the delivery space through the ClickUp connector.
  • A daily digest sent to the scrum master.
  • A live dashboard that refreshes every morning at 9.

The result

Stuck work shows up every morning, before it turns into a missed deadline.
