Tech consultancy website: cleanup, growth pages and tracking

Ongoing Webflow work for a technology consultancy, starting with a cleanup of the existing build.

The problem

The site came in with a messy build. The team needed new pages, stronger forms and proper tracking to grow.

How it went

Cleanup→CMS structure→New pages→Forms & pop-ups→Tracking

What I did

  • Cleaned up the existing build so changes are safe and quick.
  • Heavy CMS work to keep content structured and easy to update.
  • New page variants and service pages, including AI Safety & Data Security.
  • Large Finsweet CMS filters and multi-step forms.
  • Pop-ups and personalized calls to action driven by the CMS.
  • Swiper.js sliders.
  • Tracking with GA4, Google tags, Search Console and Microsoft Clarity.

Where AI helped

Most of this was hands-on Webflow work. I built some sections with Claude through the Webflow MCP.

The result

A clean site the marketing team can keep growing, with clear data on what visitors do.
