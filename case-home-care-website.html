Rosie Nightingale Homecare: cleanup, CMS and new pages

A UK home care provider's Webflow site. I took over a messy build, cleaned it up, and made new pages quick to add.

Service
Webflow
Industry
Home care
Built with
Webflow, Claude, Webflow MCP
View live site ↗

The problem

The site came from another developer. Class names were messy, so every change was slow and easy to break.

How it went

Messy build→Clean naming→CMS collections→Pages→New page via MCP

What I did

  • Cleaned up the class names into one clear, consistent system.
  • Built CMS collections for services, jobs and blog posts.
  • Rebuilt the pages on top of the clean structure.
  • Added a Supported Living page with a custom intake form.

Where AI helped

I recently built a new page with Claude through the Webflow MCP. It reused the site's existing classes and components, so the page matched the rest of the site from the start.

The result

A clean site the team can grow. Services, jobs and blog posts go in through the CMS, and new pages fit the design without extra work.
