Donor retention flows for a nonprofit

Connecting a donation platform to email, so every donor and almost-donor gets the right follow-up.

Service
Automation
Industry
Nonprofit
Built with
Fundraise Up, Zapier, Klaviyo

The problem

Fundraise Up and Klaviyo don't talk to each other. Donors and people who started a donation but left never got a follow-up.

How it works

Successful donations
Donation completed→Zapier→Klaviyo list→Thank-you flow
Abandoned donations
Donation abandoned→Zapier→Klaviyo list→Re-engagement flow
Major gifts
Donation of $5k+→Team alert→Personal note

What I built

  • Zapier flows that fire whenever someone donates or abandons a donation.
  • Each person is added to the right Klaviyo list automatically.
  • Those lists trigger Klaviyo flows: a thank-you for donors and a reminder for people who didn't finish.
  • Gifts of $5,000 or more alert the team, so they can send a personal note.

The result

Abandoned donations get a second chance, donors are thanked right away, and big donors get a personal touch. It all helps keep donors giving.
