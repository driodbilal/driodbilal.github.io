Automated fundraising reports for two nonprofits

Donation, email and ad results pulled into one sheet with a single form submission.

Service
Automation
Industry
Nonprofit
Built with
Make.com, Google Sheets
5data sources in one sheet
~10 mininstead of 30–60 min by hand
Weeklyready for every team meeting

The problem

Every week someone copied numbers from donation platforms, email and ad accounts into a sheet. It took 30 minutes to an hour each time.

How it works

Submit a form→Make.com→Fundraise Up→LaunchGood→Klaviyo→Meta Ads→Google Ads→Google Sheet

What I built

  • One form starts the whole report. No logins, no copying.
  • Make.com pulls the latest numbers from all five platforms.
  • Everything lands in one Google Sheet, ready to use for the weekly report.

The result

A job that took up to an hour now takes one form submission and about ten minutes of waiting.
